YLÉA FLORÉA — Static Website

1) Extract the ZIP completely.
2) Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3) Enter the Garden > Shop > Add to Cart or Buy Now > Create My Order Summary.
4) Copy, download or print the local shopping summary.

Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No Gmail, backend, Node.js or installation needed.
The summary is not sent to the shop and is not proof of purchase.
